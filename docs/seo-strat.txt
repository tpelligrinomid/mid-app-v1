All 90 of those people are shopping for a replacement today.Module 2: A keyword gets you a page. A pattern gets you a machine.A pattern is a set of keywords with identical intent, where Google returns the same type of page every time. Only one word changes.“[Tool A] vs [Tool B]”“Best SEO tool for [ecommerce]”“SEO consultant [city]”“[Service] [city]”“[Product type] [material]”One pattern is dozens of pages. You never pick a topic again. You pick the next row.The free way to find them is Google Suggest. Type your seed, then add a * at the end, then at the start. Each one surfaces a different batch. Google Ads’ Keyword Planner adds the volumes.The fast way is to let Claude ask the analyst.Step 6 is there for a reason. Claude will happily fill a blank column with a plausible number. The analyst exists so it never has to.Module 3: If you sell software, there are 9 page types. Write them in this order.The order is by intent and by how hard the SERP is.Alternative pages. Alternative to X, free alternative to X, cheaper alternative to X. Highest intent on the list. No company writes a page helping you leave them, so the incumbent cannot compete. Write these first.Comparison pages. X vs Y, X or Y, X vs Y vs Z. Two vendors and a pile of affiliate content is the whole field. Be honest about the rows where you lose. It is the only thing that makes the rest believable.Integration pages. Your tool + Slack, + Stripe, + Shopify. You borrow the search volume of somebody else’s brand and almost nobody fights you for it. Twelve integrations is twelve pages.Constraint pages. Without subscription, without credit card, open source. Binary filters, not opinions. If you match the constraint, you win the page outright. If you don’t, don’t write it.Pricing pages. People search price before product. Directories already sit on most of these, so do them once you have some authority.Use case pages. Tools to generate leads, to automate prospecting. You rank for the job instead of the category. Slower to land.Problem pages. The reader doesn’t know your category exists yet. Weak competition, low intent, real payoff at month six.Feature pages. Only for features you genuinely lead on. Otherwise you are publishing a directory listing about yourself.Company size pages. For freelancers, for SMEs. I would mostly skip these. They are almost always the same page with three words swapped, and Google has a name for that pattern.Local business? Your patterns are “[service] [city]” and “[category] [city]”. Decide before you write.Here is the check Paul does by hand. It is your spam defence.In March 2024 Google added scaled content abuse to its spam policies. It applies no matter whether the content came from automation, humans, or a mix of both. What it targets is large amounts of unoriginal content that gives readers little to no value.→ Volume is not a violation.
